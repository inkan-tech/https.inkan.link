## Inkan.link at CES 2025

Inkan.link took part in CES in Las Vegas for the first time in January 2025. We showed [Sealfie](https://sealf.ie/en/) at the Venetian Expo, Hall G, booth 60711. The executive takes a selfie and approves, while several attestations run in the background, with no training for the user. The subscription is €95 per user per month, with custom enterprise options, and we announced our partnership with ShareID at the show.

The launch press release, dated 27 December 2024, is published on [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/inkanlink-debuts-at-ces-2025-with-revolutionary-anti-fraud-solution-as-business-email-compromise-reaches-5b-annual-loss-302338995.html) and SecurityInfoWatch republished it on 3 January 2025. Biometric Update ran an editorial article on the show on 7 January 2025, written by Lu-Hai Liang: it quotes our founder on using a selfie to approve an important transaction ([read the article](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/ces-2025-palm-biometric-locks-korean-ai-modules-for-atms-selfie-busting-ceo-deepfakery)). These references, with our other public talks, are also on the [Sealfie press page](https://sealf.ie/en/presse/).
